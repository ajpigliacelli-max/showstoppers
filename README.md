# Resident program modules — The Bristal at Sayville

> GitHub Pages currently publishes the `docs/` folder. The files in `docs/` are the live copies — when a module changes, copy it into `docs/` too (or switch Pages to publish from the root and delete `docs/`).

Five browser modules for 45-minute resident programming. They run from a link,
need nothing installed, and play their clips in the page.

### Showstoppers! — musical movies game
https://ajpigliacelli-max.github.io/showstoppers/

Five games plus a head-to-head duel, a 61-clip library, a persistent scoreboard
and 25 sourced intermission facts. Presenter notes hide by default so the screen
can face the room.

### That Scene! — iconic movie moments
https://ajpigliacelli-max.github.io/showstoppers/thatscene.html

A 45-minute presenter-led program: ten clips across four reels, two narrated history
segments, and two games. The running order sits across the top with its clock times,
and the host script sits inside each segment — what to say before each clip, the two
history talks in full, and the answer keys. Audience Mode hides all of it so the screen
can face the room.

Clips are embedded and trimmed to start and stop where they should. Nine more verified
clips sit in the spare library. Every clip was checked live: it resolves, and its owner
allows embedding.

### The Laugh Track — classic TV montage
https://ajpigliacelli-max.github.io/showstoppers/laughtrack.html

Ten classic TV scenes back to back, about 46 minutes, advancing on their own.
I Love Lucy, The Honeymooners, Carol Burnett, Johnny Carson, Mary Tyler Moore,
Andy Griffith, All in the Family — plus eight spares to swap in.

### Silent Laughs — silent-era comedians (Reflections)
https://ajpigliacelli-max.github.io/showstoppers/silentlaughs.html

About 47 minutes, roughly 80% film. Intertitle-style title cards introduce the
Keystone Cops, Chaplin, Harold Lloyd, Buster Keaton and Laurel & Hardy; the show
stops at each card so the host can talk, and the clips after it roll on their own.
Host script sits under the player with Presenter notes on. Nine spare clips.

### Women Who Roast — comedy legends (assisted living)
https://ajpigliacelli-max.github.io/showstoppers/womenwhoroast.html

About 44 minutes, all roasts, every clip playing inside the module. Act One is the
Dean Martin Celebrity Roasts: Phyllis Diller, the 1975 Lucille Ball roast (Ruth Buzzi,
Vivian Vance, Totie Fields, Lucy) and Betty White's 1978 roast. Act Two is the cable
roasts: Betty White at Shatner, Cloris Leachman at Saget, Joan Rivers' rebuttal at her
own 2009 roast, Martha Stewart at Bieber, and Nikki Glaser. Comedy Central blocks its
own uploads from playing inside other sites, so Act Two uses fan uploads of the TV
broadcasts, news coverage and Netflix; the Netflix Nikki clip is not bleeped. Several
slots carry backup clips (`alts`) that roll if the first choice is blocked. A Heat
Check can skip the whole act. Five trivia questions, a two-act Roast Meter that
crowns a Queen of the Roast, Roast the Host, and an Encore board for extra time.
Full-screen button (or F), a clip check that tests every clip and backup, and
auto-skip if one fails mid-show.
Pop-art illustrations made in OpenArt live in `womenwhoroast-art/`.

Every clip in every module was checked live: the video resolves and its owner
allows embedding. Clips are embedded, never copied — they play from their
original uploads.
